Art & Design

Being born with a natural skill and passion for drawing has driven my aesthetic approach across the board. Whether it's crafting an intricate portrait with charcoal or architecting a robust backend system, it’s all about design. My work is showcased here as both raw art and an influence on my technological advancements.

Roar

A striking study of intensity and power, capturing the fierce expression of a lion in mid-roar.

Serenity

Contrastingly, this piece focuses on the calm, composed presence of the king of the jungle.

The Elder

A deeply textured portrait emphasizing wisdom and time, bringing out complex emotion through shading.

Innocence

Capturing pure emotion and cultural depth in the expressive eyes of an African child.

Cultural Heritage

An exploration of adornment and traditional beauty, focusing on intricate details and lighting.

Hieroglyph

A stylized interpretation inspired by ancient history and symbolic communication.

Ethereal

A surreal composition blending human form with mythological and ethereal elements.

Mechanism

A detailed tribute to iconic machinery, focusing on sharp lines and mechanical complexity.

Symbiote

An aggressive, high-contrast illustration capturing chaos and fluidity in popular culture.

Will of Fire

A tribute to anime culture, focusing on iconic character design and kinetic energy.

Shadow

The thematic counterpart piece, emphasizing darkness, contrast, and emotion.

Gaze

A minimalist, intense study of a single eye, drawing the viewer's focus to the iris and reflection.
